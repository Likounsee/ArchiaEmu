# Legal and third-party components

ArchiaEmu does not bundle commercial ROMs, BIOS images or firmware. Users are responsible for supplying content they are legally entitled to use where required.

Before adding a dependency or external core, record its upstream source, exact version or revision, license, redistribution obligations, platform support, maintenance status and known security concerns. Confirm that static linking, dynamic linking, packaging and source distribution comply with the applicable license. Preserve required notices and source offers.

The workspace currently has no third-party Rust dependencies and no C/C++ component. The workspace declares `MIT OR Apache-2.0` as package metadata; actual distribution licensing still requires adding and reviewing the corresponding repository license files before release.

Do not implement circumvention of console protections or redistribute copyrighted firmware without authorization.
